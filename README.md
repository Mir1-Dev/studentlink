# StudentLink

![StudentLink Logo](assets/images/logo.png)

**Your smart campus study companion** — A Flutter mobile app designed to help Conestoga College students manage their academic life.

## What is StudentLink?

StudentLink is a student productivity app that combines task management, location discovery, and weather tracking in one convenient place.

### Key Features

📋 **Task Management**
- Create, edit, and delete study tasks
- Set due dates and subjects
- Mark tasks as completed
- View all your tasks in one place

📍 **Study Location Finder**
- Interactive map of campus study spots
- Explore quiet zones (library, study pods, tech lab)
- Find active study areas (commons, cafeteria)
- See all locations at a glance

🌤️ **Weather Forecast**
- Current weather conditions for your campus
- Wind speed and temperature tracking
